Research

Coastal Flood Risk Under Climate Change

How high will a 100-year storm surge be at a given location, and how is that changing as the climate shifts? I'm building methods that fuse physics-based ocean simulations with historical tide gauge observations to forecast extreme coastal water levels across climate scenarios.

Dissertation in progress · Expected May 2026 · Advised by Richard Smith

Opioid Misuse Surveillance

Most opioid misuse goes unobserved in standard surveillance data. I implement and contribute to the development of Bayesian models that integrate fragmented data sources across spatial and temporal scales to estimate the size of hidden populations, and work with state health departments to turn these estimates into operational tools.

Supported by NIDA R01DA052214 (MPIs: Kline, Hepler)

Clinical Research

Embedded statistician across clinical teams in surgery, pediatrics, gerontology, and gastroenterology. Contributing statistician on the NIH-funded LEAP randomized clinical trial studying long-term anti-obesity medication effectiveness. I also apply causal inference methods to observational health records for outcomes research.

Scriabin, Étude in E major, Op. 8, No. 5 (1894)

Marked Brioso (spirited, lively). One of twelve études from Op. 8, composed when Scriabin was twenty-two and still under Chopin's influence.

Progress (Feb 2026): About a month of work. First clean playthrough, a foundation to build on.

Technique: Redistributing notes between the hands, letting them help each other to minimize large leaps.
